Cookie statement

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This statement explains the website’s use of cookies and similar technologies under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (“PECR”) and the UK GDPR.

1. Cookies and browser preferences

The website does not set cookies or run analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, web beacons, device fingerprinting or social-media scripts. Its typefaces and images are served from its own address.

Temporary session storage in this tab remembers whether the opening sequence has been shown. This setting stays in your browser and is not sent to the website as tracking data. If session storage is unavailable, the website still works without remembering it.

2. Server logs

Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider whose servers record technical information when pages are requested. These server logs are separate from the browser preference described above. See the privacy notice for details.

3. Links to other websites

Links to press, video, company and social pages leave this website. Those sites may set their own cookies, under their own policies.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Guidance is available at ico.org.uk.

5. Changes

If the website ever begins to use cookies or similar technologies, this statement will be updated before they are used and, where the law requires, your consent will be asked for first.
