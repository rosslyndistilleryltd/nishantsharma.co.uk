Media contact

Press enquiries: message Nishant on LinkedIn. Trade enquiries for the spirits: Rutland Square Spirits. Company news: rutlandsquarespirits.com/blogs/news.

About. Nishant Sharma is the founder and CEO of Rutland Square Spirits, born in Dibrugarh, Assam, and based in Edinburgh. Rutland Square Spirits makes Chai Spiced Scottish Gin, distilled by the London Dry method, and Chai Spiced Scottish Rum in Edinburgh.
