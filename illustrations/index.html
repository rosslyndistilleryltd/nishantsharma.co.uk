About the illustrations

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. How the pictures are labelled

  • Narrative illustration: a painting made for this website to interpret a chapter of the story. Each was directed, selected and edited by Nishant Sharma from family memory and personal archive references. It is not a photograph and may not show places, people or objects exactly as they were.
  • Archive photograph: a real photograph from the family or company archive.
  • Brand photography: photography of Rutland Square Spirits products and serves, supplied by the company.
  • Illustrative concept: a computer-generated concept image of Rosslyn Distillery & Estate, a proposed development. It remains subject to design development, planning permission, statutory consents and final agreements.
  • Packaging design study: a design study of proposed packaging, not a finished product.
  • Book pages: scanned pages from The Book of Rutland Square, Volume I, shown with their text.

2. People and places

The illustrations depict Nishant Sharma and his family as remembered and imagined. Other figures, rooms and street scenes are illustrative and are not intended to portray identifiable real people or exact locations. Family history in the story is oral history, as told by Nishant’s grandfather and recounted by Nishant.
